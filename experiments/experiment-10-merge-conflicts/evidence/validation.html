What the tests protect

Post-resolution scope
01

Fair priority, stable order

VIP requests receive seats first. Earlier arrivals retain their order inside each priority group; later VIP requests join the queue before standard requests.

02

Complete allocation

Every request appears exactly once in confirmed seats or the waiting list. Zero capacity queues everyone; sufficient capacity clears the queue.

03

Limits and attendee validation

Capacity boundaries of 0 and 100 are accepted. Invalid numbers, strings, duplicate IDs and incomplete attendee records are rejected.

04

Safe inputs and available seats

A frozen roster and attendee objects remain unchanged. Empty workshops and unused seats keep their expected capacity.
