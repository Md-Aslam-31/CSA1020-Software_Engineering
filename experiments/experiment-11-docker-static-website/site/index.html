SMALL BEGINNINGS, BIG PERSPECTIVE

Your night starts here.

Three simple ways to turn
looking up into a habit.

01 / OBSERVE✧

THE FAMILIAR, SEEN AGAIN

Start with the Moon.

Find a comfortable spot and take a closer look at a familiar neighbour. Notice the shapes, shadows, and edge of its light.

A small observation +
Make a quick sketch of what you see. Add the date and your own words. Return another evening and compare your notes. Never look at the Sun through binoculars or a telescope.
02 / CONNECT

PATTERNS & PERSPECTIVE

Find your own shapes.

Pick a few bright points and imagine the lines between them. Before learning the names, let your curiosity do the connecting.

A small observation +
Draw a pattern you can recognise again. Mark a nearby landmark on your page to help you find the same part of the sky next time. The artwork above is an imagined pattern.
03 / PAUSE

A QUIETER KIND OF EVENING

Make room for the dark.

Choose a safe place away from bright lights. Put your phone aside for a moment, settle in, and see what slowly comes into view.

A small observation +
Write down three things you notice: one in the sky, one on the horizon, and one nearby. Check the weather, stay aware of your surroundings, and respect local access rules.

PACK LIGHT. LOOK UP.

A good evening
needs very little.

A few essentials, a safe place, and the willingness to pause. Tick these off before you head out.

The best equipment is your attention.
