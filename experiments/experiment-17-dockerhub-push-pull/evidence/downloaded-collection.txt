MERIDIAN ATLAS
Your collection of little adventures

CHENNAI — The city beyond the rush
3 hours on foot | $12–24 USD sample budget per person | culture
  1. Begin in Mylapore: Wander the old neighbourhood lanes and notice the details in the temple architecture.
  2. Pause for filter coffee: Find a small local café and take your time over a warm cup and a simple snack.
  3. Finish by the water: Walk the Marina promenade and let the sea breeze set the pace.

KYOTO — A quieter shade of green
4 hours on foot | $18–32 USD sample budget per person | nature
  1. Follow the canal: Start with a gentle walk along the Philosopher’s Path and its changing seasonal scenery.
  2. Find a garden pause: Choose a nearby garden or green space for a quiet break and a few pages of a book.
  3. Take the back streets: Explore small neighbourhood lanes and stop for tea before heading back.
